Who's playing?

Graphics
Two players take turns on one screen · Esc or P pauses

How to play

Win

Each castle has a teddy on a throne inside its keep. Knock the other teddy off his throne. Careful: if your own teddy falls, you lose.

Turn

Every turn you get pocket money, go to the shop, then fire one shot from your spoon catapult.

Aim

Drag on the table to pull back (or use the arrow keys), then Fire! The dotted line shows how the shot starts. The draft from the window blows it off course.

Ammo

Paper balls are cheap but blow about. Erasers bounce. Marbles are dear and go straight through walls.

Money

Chores earn a coin or more for every toy not working a catapult, but the toys get grumpy. Piggy banks pay a little every turn plus 10% interest, and coins you don't spend go in them. Smash the other side's piggies: half their coins roll over to you.

Toys

Keep your toy soldiers fed with snacks and not overworked. Happy toys bring friends. Sulky toys skip shots. Unhappy toys walk over to the other castle, and if nobody is left, or they mutiny, you lose.

Build

Rebuild knocked-down blocks and the flag, add piggy banks, a back catapult that lobs over your keep, or glue to make your castle sturdier for two enemy shots.

Kitchen

Now and then the kitchen joins in: the cat walks across the table, Grandma visits, it's dinner time…
